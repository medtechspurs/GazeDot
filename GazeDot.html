Chopper Run

v0.7

Fly a helicopter through the city with your head. Small moves: turn or tilt to bank, chin up to climb, chin down to dive.

Lean back from the phone to brake. Shout “pew!” (or tap) to fire the laser.

Uses the front camera and microphone; nothing leaves your phone.

Quick calibration

You'll be asked to do these four things, about two seconds each. Hold the phone the way you'll play, and keep the movements small.

1. Look straightat the screen
2. Turn leftlike looking to your left
3. Tilt leftear toward shoulder
4. Chin up

In the game: lean back from the phone to brake and let enemies pass. Shout “pew!” or tap to fire.
